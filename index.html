Lucky Charm Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Lucky Charm is designed to provide interactive companions inside Chrome. The extension stores your selected charm and local preferences using Chrome's local extension storage.

Data we collect

Lucky Charm does not collect, sell, or transmit your browsing history, page contents, passwords, messages, or personal information to a remote server.

Website access

The extension adds its companion interface to webpages so the charm can hang from the top of the viewport and respond to user interaction. Page content is not collected or sent to Lucky Charm.

Local storage

Your selected charm and settings such as size, sound, visibility, and gentle sway are stored locally in Chrome.

Third parties

Lucky Charm does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking services in this release.

Changes

If the privacy practices change, this policy will be updated and the Chrome Web Store listing will be updated as appropriate.
